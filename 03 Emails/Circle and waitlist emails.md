### B3 ◆ Tue Dec 8, 2026
**Subject:** Christmas without the January hangover
**Subject B:** A gift to future you
**Preview:** It starts with one small number.
**Main link:** [LIBRARY LINK]

Hi [first name],

There is a particular feeling in the second week of January. The tree is down, the card statement arrives, and Christmas comes back for one more visit.

I want to help you skip that next year. The tool is a sinking fund.

A sinking fund is just money you set aside a little at a time for something you already know is coming. Christmas is the easiest one there is. It is not a surprise. It lands on the same day every year.

Here is the simple version.

After Christmas, write down what this one actually cost. Gifts, food, the extra gas, the stocking stuffers you forgot you bought. All of it.

Divide that by the number of paychecks you get in a year. That is your Christmas number.

Then move it every payday, even if it is smaller than the real number to start. Five dollars is not nothing. It is five dollars you will not be paying interest on in January.

I think of it as a gift to future you. She is tired too, and she will be so glad you did this.
