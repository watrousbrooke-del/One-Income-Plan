Wins and gratitude pages

Where small wins count. Especially the ones nobody else in the house noticed.

A one-income budget mostly runs on quiet choices. The meal you stretched, the cart you put back, the bill you paid on time without any fuss. Nobody claps for those, so they slip by and the hard weeks end up being the only ones you remember. These pages are where you catch the good ones. Ten minutes on a Sunday is plenty.
