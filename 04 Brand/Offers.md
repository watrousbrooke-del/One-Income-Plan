Not ten. Just one, picked by you." PDF, 1 page, 5 minutes.

Permission script: "You're not documenting failure, you're gathering data."
- **Sometimes called:** "the free tracker," "the free worksheet," "Free Reality Check" (nav button). (BMG, SITE)
- **Old names / earlier ideas:** "One-Income Budget Starter Kit" / "One Income Budget Starter Kit" (WB 1.1, 1.2, 1.5, 3.1, used as the freebie name before the Jan 2026 offer work); "The 5-Minute Money Check-In," "The Real Reason Your Budget Isn't Working," "Permission Slips for Your Money" (OSQ Q4 brainstorm); "One-Income Budget Reset or Payday Budget Checklist" (OSQ Q30).

## Paid: The One-Income Circle (membership)

- **Name:** The One-Income Circle (hyphenated). Called "The One Income Plan membership" in the page eyebrow. Members are "in the Circle." Do not confuse with the brand name The One Income Plan (no hyphen).
- **Price:** founding member rate $19/month, cancel anytime, or $190/year. Founding rate "locked for as long as you stay." If you cancel and return within 90 days the founding rate comes back.
- **Guarantee:** Thirty day **Peaceful Month Promise**. Use it for 30 days (run one payday reset, come to one live session, try the meal plan for two weeks); if money still feels like the same knot, email and get the first payment refunded; keep every printable. (membership.html; project memory confirms it stays as the refund policy)
- **Who:** "Built for the mom who has read every piece of budget advice on the internet and still dreads the first of the month." Household on one income or close to it.
- **What's inside (six pieces):**
  1. **The System Vault**, seven systems: The One-Income Budget System ◆ The Sinking Fund System ◆ The Frugal Family Meal System ◆ The Grocery Strategy System ◆ Simple Home Rhythms ◆ The Money Is a Tool Mindset Kit ◆ The Quick Start Implementation Guide (member page titled "What To Do First")
  2. **The Monthly Money Reset**, one live 45-minute session a month, recorded, replay library
  3. **The Circle**, private group: weekly win thread, "ask the room before you spend," meal ideas
  4. **Ask Brooke**, monthly Q&A session, post any time, answered weekly
  5. **The Printable Library**, budget pages, trackers, bill calendar, meal planners, sinking fund and debt payoff pages, new pieces monthly
  6. **Something new each month**, seasonal plans (back to school, holidays, "Christmas sinking fund, started in July"), short trainings
- **Quick Start:** "Four steps, about fifteen minutes": print two pages, watch a five-minute video, make one decision, two small things to lock it in. (members-quick-start.html). Membership page frames it as "You join and open the Quick Start... print two pages, watch a five minute walkthrough, and pick the one area you want to work on first."
- **Core routine:** a fifteen-minute payday reset, plus a Sunday meal plan of about ten minutes.
- **Founding bonuses:** rate locked for life ◆ The First 30 Days Roadmap ($47 value) ◆ a vote on what gets built next.
- **Value stack on the page:** all seven Bundle systems $197 ◆ Budget Toolkit $27 ◆ 12 live resets $228 ◆ 12 months of printables $108 ◆ Ask Brooke included ◆ The Circle "not sold anywhere" ◆ 30 Days Roadmap $47 ◆ total "$607" first-year value. Flag: the page says "Those are the real prices these pieces sell for on their own," but the Toolkit and Bundle have no sales page on the site today.
- **Previous buyers:** Toolkit or Bundle buyers get what they spent credited toward membership.
- **Not for:** investing advice, credit repair, making money quickly, done-for-you, two full incomes wanting wealth building, tip collectors, families for whom $19 would take food off the table.
- **History:** Brooke agreed to a membership add-on in Aug 2025 (project memory). Brooke's own early answers favored membership: "I think my niche requires it... I want to create reaccurring income." (WB 1.2). The Jan 2026 offer docs did not include a membership; the Circle effectively packages the Bundle's seven systems plus the Signature Program's community and live support at a monthly price.

## Brooke's boundaries for every offer (OSQ Q36)
No pressure, urgency, or fear. No countdown timers. No extremes, no shame. Nothing requiring her to be constantly available. No trend-chasing or flashy promises. "My offers should always communicate: You're capable. You're learning. You're allowed to go at your own pace."
