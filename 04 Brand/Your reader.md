## A day in her life

"She wakes up already feeling behind. Before her feet even hit the floor, she is mentally tallying bills, groceries, and everything that needs to be stretched just a little further this month." (BMG 2.1)

Brooke's scene: "Her home is loving but sometimes chaotic. There are bills on the counter, a half-finished meal plan on the fridge, and Amazon boxes she regrets ordering. She balances family life, work or side gigs, and constant responsibilities, and finances always feel like a weight in the background." (WB 2.5)

Recurring triggers (WB 2.5):
1. "Grocery trips that cost more than planner" [sic, means planned]
2. "Unexpected bills that derail the budget"
3. "Talking about money with her partner and feeling tension or guilt"

Bad day: "It hits her when she checks her account and sees another overdraft. She feels embarrassed, frustrated, and ashamed to start over again. Her confidence drops, and she avoids talking about money because she doesn't want to admit she's struggling." (WB 2.5)

## Objections

Voiced (BMG 2.5), with the brand's response angle:
- "We can't afford to spend money on a course right now." (saves $50 in month one, pays for itself)
- "I have tried budgeting before and it never sticks." (the plan was the problem, not you)
- "My spouse would not be on board with this." (reduces tension, spouses notice the peace first)
- "I do not have time for another thing." (built for five quiet minutes, not five free hours)
- "There is so much free advice online, why pay?" (free gives information, this gives a system)

Brooke's list (WB 3.2): "How can I afford this?" (tight cash flow; fear of wasting money again) ◆ "I don't have time." (burnout; evening chaos with kids) ◆ "I'm not strong enough to stick to it." (low self-trust) ◆ "Where do I even start?" (too many moving parts) ◆ "Will this work for us?" (different schedules, diets, stores)

Biggest hesitation: "I'll buy it and still not follow through." Quiet doubts: "What if I quit again?" "What if this is different but I'm not?" (WB 3.2)

Unvoiced: afraid that even if she tries, it still won't work for her; fear of getting her hopes up; worry that simplifying means being judged. (BMG Q175)

Invisible filters (WB 3.2): "Does she get me?" ◆ "Will this work for someone like me?" ◆ "Is this a good use of my money?" ◆ "Will I be embarrassed if I fail again?" ◆ "Do I have it in me to follow through this time?"

## Spending and buying

- "Value-first; sale shopper; splurges on kids, birthdays, or small comforts when stressed." Triggers: "Kid needs, social events, 'we deserve a treat,' emergencies." Spending seasons: back-to-school, holidays, summer. (WB 3.2)
- Often a coupon-clipper and deal-watcher; spends when it will save money, time, or stress long-term; frames purchases as "This will help our family long-term." Won't spend on flashy upgrades. (OSQ Q27)
- Buys when "value is clear and steps are laid out; loves bundles and templates." (WB 3.2)
- Timeline: "1 to 2 weeks for toolkits; 2 to 3 weeks for courses." Needs "several gentle touches + a visible win." Triggers: "Someone like her succeeding; 'pays for itself' math." Consults "Partner, trusted creators, her journal." (WB 3.2)

## What she needs to trust

- To feel "safe, understood, and not judged" before she takes advice. (BMG Q174)
- Credibility = "You've lived it; you show the messy middle." Proof she believes: "Realistic demos, screenshots, weekly wins." (WB 3.2)
- Turn-offs: "Hype, shaming, rigid rules, perfectionism." (WB 3.2)
- Clichés she hates: "Just stop buying lattes," "You're not trying hard enough," "Anyone can do it." (WB 3.2)
- Who she follows: "women who are real, practical, and authentic people who live what they teach, not influencers selling a fantasy." (WB 2.5)
- Tone she likes: "Warm, practical, encouraging, no-shame tough love." (WB 3.2)
- Content she saves: "Pantry checklists, 7-day meal plans, Aldi hauls, before/after budgets, printable trackers." (WB 3.2)
- Post she'd screenshot for a friend: "Before/after grocery receipt + the exact 7-meal plan that made it drop." (WB 3.2)
- Copy that makes her whisper "this is me": "You're not bad with money, you're burned out on systems that don't fit family life." (WB 3.2)
