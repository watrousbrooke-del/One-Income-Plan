## 1. The brand in one breath

The One Income Plan by Frugal Home Lover is a faith friendly personal finance brand for one income families. It is built on Brooke's own story: paying off $34,000 in debt on one regular paycheck while staying home with her children.

The reader is a stay at home mom who feels overwhelmed and quietly ashamed about money, and who is hopeful that something can finally work. Every image should feel like it was made for her, not at her.

The feeling every image must carry:
◆ Warm, steady, sincere. Never slick, never cold.
◆ Real life, not a catalog. Lived in kitchens, worn notebooks, actual daylight.
◆ Hopeful, not perfect. Progress over polish.
◆ Calm and uncluttered, with one small spark of joy.
◆ Faith friendly in warmth and tone, never preachy or overtly religious in imagery.

THE IMAGE CHECK: Before you use any image, ask: does this look like a real moment in a real home that a tired mom on a tight budget could actually recognize as her own? If it looks like a finance stock photo, start again.

## 3. Retired colors ◆ do not use

The original 2025 palette still appears inside older project files, including the Offer Ladder page and the first lead magnet brief. It has been replaced. If an image generator picks these up from an old file, correct it.

| Color name | Hex | Replaced by |
|---|---|---|
| Old sage (retired) | #6B705C | Deep sage #2E3E2F and sage #5C6E47 |
| Old tan (retired) | #D4A373 | Warm tan #C28954 |
| Old cream (retired) | #FEFAE0 | Cream #FBF4D7 |
| Old muted olive (retired) | #A3A380 | Soft sage #8B9E75 |

Colors that break the brand. These do not belong anywhere in an image, not even as a small detail:
◆ Corporate or navy blue of any shade. It reads bank, not kitchen.
◆ Cool gray, silver, chrome, or anything metallic and modern.
◆ Neon, fluorescent, or high saturation brights.
◆ Pure black. Charcoal only.
◆ Hot pink, purple, or teal. These read as a different brand entirely.
◆ Cold white light, blue hour, or fluorescent office lighting.

How much of each color, a rough recipe:
◆ 60 percent cream, warm cream, pale tan, or white. The ground.
◆ 25 percent sage in its various depths. Plants, textiles, deep bands, type.
◆ 10 percent warm tan and wood. Furniture, kraft paper, leather, bread, coffee.
◆ 5 percent coral and gold together. One mug, one flower, one badge, one ray of light. Never more.

## 4. Typography and the logo

| Font | Usual color | Where it belongs |
|---|---|---|
| Georgia | #2E3E2F | Everything structural. Headlines, body copy, buttons, navigation. Bold Georgia in deep sage is the brand headline. |
| Caveat | #DD6B4E | The handwritten accent. Emotional asides, the tagline, greetings such as Hi, friend, sticker text, and "by Frugal Home Lover" in the logo. Warm and human, used sparingly. |
| DM Serif Display | #D4A83F | Numbers and statistics only. $34,000, 15 minutes, step numerals. Never body copy. |

The logo lockup:
◆ A small coral dot, then The One Income Plan set in bold Georgia, deep sage.
◆ Beside or beneath it, by Frugal Home Lover set in Caveat, deep coral. This line always appears with the name on the website header, so the brand stays tied to Frugal Home Lover and stands apart from similar names.
◆ The tagline spend with intention. save with purpose. stays, set in Caveat, used in footers and seals, not in the logo.
◆ No icon, no illustration, no piggy bank, no coin, no leaf is ever added to the lockup. The coral dot is the only mark.
◆ The logo sits on cream or white. It may sit on deep sage if the wordmark is switched to cream and the dot stays coral.

If an image needs text in it: zero em dashes and zero en dashes, anywhere, ever. Use commas, periods, or the diamond separator. Never use these words: hustle, boss babe, girlboss, manifest, abundance mindset. Sentence case, plain honest words, warm and steady. If Brooke would not say it over coffee, it does not go in the image.

## 5. Photography and image style

Every image should look like a real moment someone happened to catch, lit by an actual window.

Light:
◆ Natural daylight only. Soft morning light or late golden hour.
◆ Window light from the side, gentle shadows, warm color temperature.
◆ No flash, no studio lighting, no hard rim light, no artificial color gels.

Composition:
◆ Shallow depth of field with a soft, warm background.
◆ Generous negative space, especially in cream or pale tan, so copy can sit on top.
◆ Slightly imperfect framing. A little off center feels more honest than perfectly symmetrical.
◆ One clear subject. Uncluttered but lived in.

Setting:
◆ A modest family kitchen, a small dining table, a sunny corner of a real home.
◆ Signs of actual life: children's art on the fridge, a worn notebook, a chipped mug, a small plant.
◆ Never a luxury kitchen, marble island, designer showroom, or open plan mansion.

People:
◆ A warm, genuine mom in her thirties. Comfortable clothes in sage, cream, tan, or soft coral.
◆ Real expressions. Mid laugh, a soft smile, a quiet focused moment. Never a posed camera smile.
◆ Hands doing real things: writing, holding a mug, sorting envelopes, cooking with a child.
◆ Children may appear naturally in the background or beside her. Faces can be partly turned away.

| Always | Never |
|---|---|
| Warm natural window light | Stock photo posing or fake laughter |
| Real, unposed, slightly imperfect moments | Piggy banks, coin jars, stacks of cash, dollar signs |
| Modest, lived in family homes | Calculators, charts, spreadsheets, tickers, graphs |
| Handwritten notebooks and pen on paper | Suits, offices, handshakes, boardrooms |
| Cream, sage, tan base with a coral spark | Luxury kitchens, marble, designer interiors |
| Soft focus flowers, plants, a candle, a coffee mug | Cold blue, gray, neon, or metallic tones |
| Calm faces with quiet hope on them | Shame based imagery: stress, tears, hands in face, red ink |

## 6. Graphic elements and shapes

◆ Hand drawn squiggle underline in coral beneath a key headline phrase. Loose, imperfect, one continuous wave.
◆ Four point sparkle star in gold or coral, scattered sparingly on deep sage bands.
◆ Rotated sticker badges. Small rectangles tilted two or three degrees, gold or coral fill, white uppercase Georgia text, hard offset shadow beneath.
◆ Pill shaped buttons with a solid three pixel hard shadow underneath in the darker shade of the same color.
◆ Cards with a twelve pixel corner radius, white fill, soft sage tinted shadow.
◆ Circular color wash glows in coral blush or soft gold behind hero subjects.
◆ The diamond glyph as the only separator mark. It replaces every dash.
◆ Confetti bursts in coral, gold, sage, warm tan, and soft coral for celebration moments.

Illustration style, if illustration is needed:
◆ Flat, warm, and simple. Slightly textured, like screen print or gouache.
◆ Palette locked to the four families. No outlines in black, only deep sage.
◆ No cartoon mascots, no generic vector business people, no 3D renders.
