Your rhythm

Running it, week to week

Most of the business runs itself. This is the short list that is yours.

every Tuesday
  1. Copy the Tuesday newsletter template in Flodesk.
  2. Fill in the blanks: one story, one tip, one link.
  3. Send it to your list. On waitlist email Tuesdays, skip the waitlist segment.
every month
  1. 1st: send Members 1, the this month email.
  2. Update /members-this-month with the theme and printable.
  3. 7 days before the session: send Members 2.
  4. Second Thursday, 8pm ET: host the session, then send Members 3 that day.
  5. Next day: post the replay and send Members 4.
as it comes
  1. Answer Ask Brooke questions, then post them anonymously to the archive.
  2. Reply to replies. Days 1, 3 and 6 of the library emails ask for one.
  3. Pin each new blog post on Pinterest.
  4. Collect testimonials, with permission.
