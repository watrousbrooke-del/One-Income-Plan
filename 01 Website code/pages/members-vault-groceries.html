The Grocery Strategy System

Set the number before you walk in, not after you check out. The fastest visible win in the vault.

Groceries are the one big bill you get to decide every single week.

You cannot shop around on rent this month. You can on groceries. That makes this the line where a little effort shows up in your account the fastest.

The whole system rests on one idea: decide the number before you walk into the store, write it at the top of your list, and keep a running total as you go. When you know the number, you stop finding out at the register.

How to do it

Setting your number, and keeping it.

Fifteen minutes to set up, then two minutes before every trip.

1

Find out what you actually spend.

Add up every grocery receipt or card charge from the last four weeks, including the quick trips for milk. Divide by four. That is your real weekly number today.

8 minutes
2

Set your target a little lower.

Not drastically. Ten percent below your real number is a good first target. The worksheet works it out for you. You can lower it again next month.

2 minutes
3

Write the number at the top of the list.

Every trip, on the Cheat Sheet, before you leave the house. Shop from the meal plan, not from the aisles.

1 minute
4

Keep a running total.

Round each item up to the next dollar and add it down the side as it goes in the cart. Rounding up builds in a little cushion for tax and keeps the maths easy in your head.

As you shop
5

When you go over, put something back.

Not the vegetables. Look at the snacks and extras first. No guilt, no drama. The total is a decision, not a test.

As you shop
6

Write down the gap after every trip.

Your number, the receipt, the difference. After four weeks you will see exactly where the extra goes.

1 minute
Store by store

How to shop each kind of store.

General rules that hold up almost everywhere. Prices change, so let your price book be the judge.

discount grocers

Aldi and stores like it

  • Best for staples: milk, eggs, bread, rice, canned goods
  • Bring a bag and a quarter for the cart
  • Check the weekly specials aisle, but only buy what is on your list
big box

Walmart and Target

  • Use the app to check prices before you go
  • Store brands are usually as good and much cheaper
  • Pickup orders stop impulse buys before they start
regular grocery

Kroger, Meijer and similar

  • Shop the weekly ad and the digital coupons in the app
  • Loss leaders on the front page are the real deals
  • Avoid the end caps, they are rarely the best price
warehouse clubs

Costco and Sam’s

  • Only worth it for things you use fast and have room to store
  • Run the Bulk Buying Calculator before you assume it is cheaper
  • Go with a list and a number, never to browse
dollar stores

Dollar General and similar

  • Sometimes good for spices, cleaning supplies, party things
  • Often more expensive per ounce for food
  • Check the unit price, the small size is how they win
everywhere

The rules that always hold

  • Shop once a week, every extra trip costs more than you think
  • Never shop hungry, and leave the kids home when you can
  • Look high and low on the shelf, eye level is the expensive spot
Setting your number

Setting your weekly grocery number

Brooke is recording this one. Until it is up, everything you need is on this page, written out in the steps above.

done
when

You have come in at or under your number for four weeks.

Not every trip. Four weeks overall. That is when the number has become real. Then move on to Simple Home Rhythms.

When it slips

The questions everyone asks about this one.

My number feels impossibly low.

Then raise it. A number you actually hit teaches you far more than one you blow every week. Start where you are, then lower it ten percent at a time.

Does the number include things like diapers and toilet paper?

Decide once, and keep it the same every week. Most families find it easier to keep household items in a separate number, so the grocery total only measures food.

Is coupon clipping worth it?

Only if you enjoy it. Shopping from a plan with a number at the top saves more than coupons for most families. Use the store app coupons, since they take a minute, and skip the rest if it stresses you out.

We went way over this week. Now what?

Write down the gap and look at what pushed it over. One expensive week is information. Next week, plan one more pantry dinner and carry on.
